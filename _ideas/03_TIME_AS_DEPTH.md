# Time as a Spatial Axis

## Concept

Posts on the sphere sit at a depth that reflects their age. Newer items come
forward toward the camera; older ones recede into the background. The user
reads chronology by *moving* through the scene, and the site visibly
accumulates as the long-running record grows — making PURPOSE #3 (a
persistent home for voice) legible in the geometry itself rather than in a
date stamp.

## Principles advanced / tensioned

- Advances **§1 (exploration over navigation)**: depth becomes a thing to
  push through rather than scroll through.
- Advances **§3 (scene amplifies the brand)**: the accumulating record is
  the brand's claim made visible.
- Tensions with **§6 (stable spatial geography)** along the depth axis only
  — angular position stays fixed, so a returning visitor still finds the
  writing where they left it; it's just slightly further back than last
  time. The depth shift should be slow enough to feel like erosion, not
  reflow.
