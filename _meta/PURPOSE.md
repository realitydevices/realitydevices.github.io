# Reality Devices — Purpose

This document explains *why* this site exists, who it's for, and what the
brand stands for. It is the foundation for `_ideas/00_MANIFESTO.md` (design
principles) and any individual idea proposals.

If you are an engineer (human or agent) about to propose or build something
here, read this first.

## The thesis

"Reality Devices" is not an atmospheric name. It is a stated philosophy:

> Defining, naming, and making things happen is what makes things happen.

The brand is the label for all the *devices* the maintainer uses to construct
the reality he wants — consulting, reputation-building, writing in public, the
(separate) plant nursery, and this site itself. Each is a deliberate
instrument; the brand binds them under a single posture of agency through
construction.

When proposing or building, this is the spine. Anything that makes the brand
feel less *intentional* or less *constructed* is suspect.

## What this site is for, in priority order

1. **The brand's primary public surface.** It articulates the philosophy and
   holds the vision. A visitor who lands here knowing nothing about the brand
   should leave with a sense of what it stands for.

2. **A professional surface for the maintainer.** Platform Lead / Principal
   Engineer, made legible to a Brisbane audience that does not currently see
   his work (he has been remote, working for a foreign company, since 2013).
   Thinking is exposed — tech (data systems, architecture, AI) and
   organisational/team systems — not just titles.

3. **A persistent home for voice.** Distilling and refining ideas in public,
   including ideas that will not all turn out to be good. The value is the
   long-running record, not any individual post.

4. **Optionality for consulting.** The business is GST-registered and ready,
   but not currently active. The site should be credible enough to *receive*
   an opportunity, not optimised to *convert* one. Do not make it a sales
   site.

## What this site is *not*

- **Not a hub for everything the maintainer does.** The plant nursery and any
  other ventures get their own surfaces. This site may be referenced from
  those (in fine print on theirs), but they are not represented here as
  sections or features.
- **Not a personal blog or rant page.** This is a public professional
  presence. Personal life, family, and day-to-day venting do not belong here.
- **Not apolitical by accident.** Apolitical and inoffensive *by design*, but
  opinionated and distinctive in tone.
- **Not a corporate / LinkedIn clone.** "Professional" means appropriate and
  considered, not bland. The site is allowed — encouraged — to feel
  distinctive in form and voice.

## Audience, ranked

1. **Brisbane professional field** — recruiters, employers, colleagues, and
   potential business partners. The primary audience and the reason the site
   needs to be locally legible.
2. **Broader internet professional readers** — anyone who finds the writing
   and engages with it.
3. **The maintainer himself** — as a persistent record of thinking over time.

When designing or writing, optimise for #1. Do not trade #1 against #2 or #3.

## What success looks like

- Voice — a recognisable register that compounds across posts.
- Engagement — occasional, meaningful, not high-volume.
- Inbound — occasional interest in the person, the work, or the writing;
  occasional opportunities that would not have existed without this surface.

Explicitly **not** success: traffic numbers, virality, follower counts,
SEO-driven content. If a feature would optimise for those at the cost of the
above, it is the wrong feature.

## Content scope

**In scope:**
- Technology — data systems, architecture, AI.
- Organisational philosophy, team systems, ways of working.
- Adjacent professional thinking that fits a public voice.
- Less-LinkedIn writing where it serves the voice (the register can be
  broader than LinkedIn-style posts, but stays within the professional
  bracket).

**Out of scope:**
- Personal life, family, daily venting.
- Political commentary.
- The plant nursery and any other separate ventures.
